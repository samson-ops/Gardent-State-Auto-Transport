Edison Car Shipping

Car shipping in Edison, New Jersey, done right

Our Edison office on Lincoln Highway (Route 27) puts Garden State Auto Transport at the crossroads of Central New Jersey. Edison is the largest township in Middlesex County, home to more than 100,000 residents, and it is wrapped by the highways car haulers use every day: Route 1, I-287, the Garden State Parkway, and the New Jersey Turnpike at exit 10. That access translates directly into faster pickups and more competitive pricing for Edison customers, because carriers heading south on I-95 or west on I-78 pass within minutes of your door.

Edison is a relocation town. Corporate campuses along Route 1 and in Raritan Center bring in employees from across the country and send them on again a few years later. The township's large South Asian community ships cars to and from family in Texas, California, Georgia, and Illinois. Students come and go from Rutgers in nearby New Brunswick and Piscataway. And the dealership row on Route 1 generates a constant stream of vehicles bought by out-of-state buyers who need them delivered. Our Edison team handles all of it with one phone call.

Call our Edison office at (848) 266-5532 or use the instant quote tool above. You'll get a real, all-in price, not an estimate that changes at pickup, and a dispatcher who answers the phone.

FAQ

Edison auto transport questions, answered

How much does it cost to ship a car from Edison, NJ?

From Edison, a sedan to Dallas, TX typically runs $1,175–$1,400 on an open carrier, and Houston, TX about $1,200–$1,425. Larger vehicles, enclosed transport, and peak season add to that. The quote tool on this page prices your exact route in about 30 seconds.

Do you pick up in Metuchen, Woodbridge, Iselin?

Yes. Our Edison office covers all of Middlesex County and the surrounding towns, including Metuchen, Woodbridge, Iselin, Colonia. If a full-size hauler can't reach your street, the driver arranges a nearby meeting point at no charge.

Is my car insured on the way from Edison?

Yes. Every provider we refer Edison customers to uses carriers with active cargo insurance and FMCSA authority, verified before your vehicle is assigned. Ask for the certificate before pickup; the inspection report documents the car's condition at both ends.

Open or enclosed from Edison?

Open transport is right for most Edison vehicles and costs 40 to 60 percent less. Enclosed is worth it for luxury, exotic, classic, and low-clearance cars, or if you'd rather the car not see weather on the way to Dallas, TX.

Can you ship a car that doesn't run from Edison?

Yes. Choose "not operable" in the quote tool and a winch-equipped carrier is assigned. The vehicle must roll, steer, and brake; if it doesn't, tell us and we'll arrange the right equipment. The surcharge is included in your price.

Which Garden State office is closest to me if I'm not in Edison?

Our Woodbridge and Princeton offices cover the rest of this region, and all eleven New Jersey locations share one dispatch team, so pricing and carriers are the same wherever you call.
