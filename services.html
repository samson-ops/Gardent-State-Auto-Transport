Choosing a Service

Which service is right for you?

Most people need open transport. It is the standard for daily drivers, SUVs, trucks, and vans, it is how dealerships receive new cars, and it costs 40 to 60 percent less than enclosed. If your car is worth less than roughly $75,000 and you are not worried about a week of road exposure, choose open.

Choose enclosed for luxury, exotic, classic, and collector vehicles, anything with low ground clearance, a fresh paint job or wrap, or sentimental value you don't want to think about. Enclosed trailers are hard-sided, often liftgate-equipped, and carry fewer cars with more attention to each one.

Door-to-door is included with every shipment; you never need to drive to a terminal. State-to-state and cross-country describe the distance, not a different service; pricing simply scales with mileage.

Expedited is for a fixed date: a closing, a flight, a lease ending. Snowbird is seasonal round-trip shipping with both legs booked at once. Military shipping includes a discount and PCS-friendly scheduling. Auction and dealer transport handles pickup from auction lots and dealerships, including gate passes and release paperwork.

Not sure? Get a quote for open transport first. You can switch to enclosed or add expedited service before you book, and your dispatcher will tell you honestly which one you actually need.
